# Survey outreach — status as of 2026-08-06

## Voice rule for every pitch *(operator direction, 2026-08-06)*

All three open drafts were rewritten today to match the operator's own sent
messages rather than a press-release register. The pattern, taken from the two
emails the operator actually sent (Nonprofit Tech for Good, Jeff Brooks):

- **Subject is plain: "Intro + <what the data is about>."** No statistic in the
  subject line, no "New data:" framing, no number-as-headline. The operator's
  own two were "Intro and Nonprofit Software Spend Survey" and "Intro + New data
  point on donor databases."
- **Open with the introduction, every time:** "My name is Charles, and I recently
  started nonprofitsoftwareguide.com."
- **First person singular.** "I just ran a survey," never "we surveyed." The site
  is one person and the emails should not pretend otherwise.
- **URL sits on its own line with no trailing punctuation.** A comma after a URL
  gets swallowed by autolinkers, which is what produced the `/benchmarks,` hits
  in GA4 on 2026-08-04.
- **Close by offering, not asking:** "Free to use with attribution, happy to
  answer questions or pull a different cut of the data if something else would be
  more useful for <their thing>."
- **Sign-off:** Best, / Charles / Nonprofit Software Guide /
  nonprofitsoftwareguide.com

Every draft below follows this. Nothing is ever sent from here — the drafts sit
in Gmail and the operator sends them.

"shared with this session" — reading a file's contents (even via an explicit
`@`-mention) doesn't satisfy that, and neither does a scratchpad copy. Every

hand: open Drafts, click the paperclip, pick
