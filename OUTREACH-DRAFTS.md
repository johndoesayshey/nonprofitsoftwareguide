## The three numbers to lead with

Verified against `src/data/survey-2026.json` on 2026-08-05:

1. **36% of nonprofits raising under $250K have no donor database at all.**
   Not a slow CRM, not a bad one — nothing. (`pctZero.donor-crm.under-250k`)
2. **Software spend scales 37x between the smallest and largest nonprofits
   surveyed** — $2,611/yr mean at the smallest band, $96,815/yr at the
   largest. (`meanTotal`)
3. **Donation processing, not the CRM, is the single biggest software line at
   every size** — 39% of the total budget for the smallest shops, and
   $30,824/yr by itself at the top end. (`mean.donation-processing`)

Any one of these can lead, depending on the outlet's beat. #1 is the most
"ouch, that's my sector" stat for general fundraising press. #3 is the one
that surprises people who assume the CRM is the center of the stack. #2 is
the cleanest single number for a subject line.

>   and $30,824/yr on its own for the largest.
