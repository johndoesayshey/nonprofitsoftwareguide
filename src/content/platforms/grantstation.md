[GrantStation](/go/grantstation) is a flat-fee grant database covering private, corporate, and government funders, with weekly alert newsletters. Through TechSoup, eligible nonprofits pay about $199 a year, sometimes closer to $150 on seasonal promotions. At that price it's the easiest yes in grant research: one found funder pays for a decade of membership.

## Who it fits

The shop building its first funder list. You have more time than budget, you're comfortable running your own searches, and you keep your own deadline calendar. GrantStation gives you coverage and a steady drip of new opportunities through its alert emails while you build the program.

## Pricing

| Route | Price |
|---|---|
| TechSoup member offer | ~$199/yr (promos near $150) |
| Direct membership | Higher; check TechSoup eligibility first |

## How it compares

[Instrumentl](/go/instrumentl) adds funder matching and a full deadline pipeline for roughly ten times the price, which makes sense at ten times the application volume. [Candid](/go/candid) has deeper data on any single foundation. GrantStation's lane is breadth per dollar: the most funders for the least money, with you doing the sorting.

## Bottom line

At the TechSoup rate this is a low-risk purchase for any shop starting or restarting a grant program. Pair it with a simple deadline calendar from day one, and [check the current membership offer](/go/grantstation) before buying direct.
