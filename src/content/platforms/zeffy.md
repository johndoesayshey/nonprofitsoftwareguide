Zeffy is donation-processing software that is genuinely free to nonprofits — no platform fee, no processing fee — funded instead by optional tips donors can add at checkout. For a budget-constrained shop, it is worth it precisely because the math is unbeatable: you keep 100% of every gift. The tradeoff is a thinner integration ecosystem than paid tools.

## Who it fits

Grassroots and small shops where every dollar of fee matters, and anyone who bristles at paying a percentage to collect donations. If your objection to online giving has been the fees, Zeffy removes the objection.

## What to watch

The free model depends on donors tipping Zeffy — that's how it stays free, and it's worth understanding before you adopt it. Integrations are lighter than paid processors, so a complex stack may need workarounds. There's no affiliate program by design; recommending Zeffy where it genuinely fits is what keeps the paid recommendations on this site credible.
