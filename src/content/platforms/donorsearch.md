DonorSearch is prospect-research software that leans hard on demonstrated philanthropy — who has actually given, and to whom — rather than wealth indicators alone. It is worth it for shops that trust giving history as the better predictor and want scores flowing into their CRM. Like all screening, it earns its keep only where there is a major-gifts program to use it.

## Who it fits

Development teams that already run donor cultivation and want to prioritize by proven generosity. If your CRM is on DonorSearch's integration list, the scores land where your gift officers already work, which is most of the value.

## What to watch

Pricing is quote-based and annual. The integration matters: without a CRM to receive the scores, you're exporting spreadsheets and losing half the benefit. Confirm your CRM is supported and get current contract terms in writing.
