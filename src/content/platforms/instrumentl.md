Instrumentl is grant-research software that finds funders matched to your work, then tracks deadlines and reporting in one place. It is worth it for a shop that spends real hours hunting for prospects in scattered databases — it turns that into a managed pipeline. It is not worth it if your funding is mostly renewals from funders you already know.

## Who it fits

The sweet spot is a shop with grant ambition but no dedicated researcher: an ED who writes grants between everything else, or a one-to-three-person development team. The matching does the prospecting a researcher would, and the deadline tracker does the project management a spreadsheet does badly.

## What to watch

Pricing is per seat and billed annually, so cost scales with your team — confirm the current number before you budget it. The value is front-loaded onto the profile you build: a vague profile returns generic matches. Budget an afternoon to set it up properly or you will underuse it.
