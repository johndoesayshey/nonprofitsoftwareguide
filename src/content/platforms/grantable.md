[Grantable](/go/grantable) is AI grant-writing software: it drafts proposal responses from your past applications and program language, so a two-hour application becomes a forty-minute edit. There's a free plan, paid tiers start at $50 a month flat, and nonprofits with budgets under $500k get 50% off the first year.

## Who it fits

The small shop where one person writes every grant between everything else they do. For a grassroots organization, this is the modern answer to the writing bottleneck: cheaper than a contract grant writer, faster than starting from a blank page, and it gets better as it learns your organization's language.

## Pricing

| Plan | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Free | $0 |
| Starter | $50 |
| Pro | $150 |

Flat pricing, no per-seat fees. The small-nonprofit discount halves the first year.

## How it compares

Grantable writes; [Instrumentl](/go/instrumentl) finds and manages. They solve different halves of the grant problem, and a growing shop can reasonably run both. If your bottleneck is finding funders, start with Instrumentl or [GrantStation](/go/grantstation). If you know your funders and drown in drafting, start here.

## Bottom line

The free plan makes this a no-risk trial: load two or three of your best past proposals and draft one real application with it. If the draft saves you an afternoon, the Starter tier pays for itself on the first grant cycle. [Try Grantable free](/go/grantable) and judge the drafts yourself.
