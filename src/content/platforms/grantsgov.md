Grants.gov is the free federal system for finding and applying to U.S. federal grants. Every shop pursuing federal money uses it — it is the system of record, not an optional tool. It is worth exactly what it costs, which is nothing. It only covers federal funders, so it is one input to a research stack, never the whole thing.

## Who it fits

Any organization going after federal funding, at any size. Because it is free and mandatory for federal applications, the real question is not whether to use it but how to pair it with a foundation-research tool that covers everything Grants.gov doesn't.

## What to watch

Registration runs through SAM.gov and is notoriously slow — start weeks ahead of any deadline. The search is functional, not pleasant. Treat Grants.gov as the federal layer of your research, with foundation and state coverage handled elsewhere.
