Givebutter is a free fundraising platform spanning donations, events, auctions, and peer-to-peer campaigns, funded by optional donor tips. It is worth it for a shop that runs campaigns and events and wants one polished, free system instead of several paid ones. If all you need is a recurring-donation form, its breadth is more than the job requires.

## Who it fits

Event- and campaign-driven shops: galas, giving days, peer-to-peer drives. Givebutter's donor-facing experience is genuinely modern, and doing it all for free on one platform is a strong pitch for a small, active shop.

## What to watch

Like Zeffy, it's tip-funded — donors are prompted to tip Givebutter, and processor fees still apply; confirm who covers them. The platform shines for events and campaigns, so weigh whether you need that range.
