Little Green Light is a low-cost donor CRM that punches above its price: flexible records, real reporting, and mailing tools without the enterprise bill. It is worth it for a small shop that wants capability over polish and is watching every dollar. It looks dated and the vendor is small, but the value is real.

## Who it fits

Budget-conscious small organizations that have outgrown a spreadsheet but can't justify a premium CRM. If your priority is "capable and cheap" over "sleek," Little Green Light is a long-standing favorite for good reason.

## What to watch

The interface won't win awards, and as a bootstrapped vendor its integration list and support are leaner than Bloomerang's or Neon's.
