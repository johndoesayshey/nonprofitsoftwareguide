Little Green Light is the donor CRM that wins on arithmetic: $45/month covers up to 2,500 constituents with unlimited users, seven tiers run $45–$135/month, and there are no contracts or setup fees. When fundraisers on a budget compare notes, LGL is the recurring answer — "does what a small shop needs without enterprise cost or complexity" is the sentiment, and switchers from bigger systems routinely report paying a third of their old bill.

## Who it fits

Small organizations graduating from spreadsheets, and lean teams that would rather have capability than polish. LGL's flexible custom fields and reporting punch well above the price; its user community is famously loyal for exactly that reason. If your priority is "capable and cheap" over "sleek," this is the category's honest answer.

## What it costs

| Constituents | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Up to 2,500 | $45 |
| Up to 5,000 | $60 |
| Scales by tier to 50,000 | up to $135 |
| Each +10,000 above 50k | +$15/mo |

Annual prepay takes 10% off. Payment processing (~2.2% + 30¢) goes to the processor, not LGL.

## The honest comparison

Against [Bloomerang](/platforms/bloomerang/): Bloomerang is friendlier and stronger on retention dashboards; LGL is nearly half the price and more flexible per dollar. Against [DonorDock](/platforms/donordock/): DonorDock guides your daily follow-up; LGL assumes you know what you're doing. The dated interface is the real tradeoff — teams that need software to look inviting before staff will use it should weigh that seriously, because the CRM you keep updated beats the cheaper one you don't.

## What to watch

Integrations are thinner than the big platforms — check your email tool and processor connect before committing. There's no affiliate program here; we recommend it anyway because leaving out the best-value option would make every other recommendation on this site look bought.
