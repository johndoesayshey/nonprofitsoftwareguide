[WealthEngine](/go/wealthengine) is wealth-screening software focused on wealth-indicator modeling: fast capacity estimates across large lists, built from assets, real estate, and market data. Pricing is quoted per organization; reported figures start around $5,000 a year plus implementation. Its lane is scale, sorting a big file quickly by estimated capacity.

## Who it fits

Organizations with large donor files and a segmentation problem: tens of thousands of records that need sorting into "worth a gift officer's time" and "keep in the newsletter." That first-pass capacity cut is what WealthEngine is built for, and it does it fast.

## Pricing

| Item (reported) | Approx. price |
|---|---|
| Single-seat subscription | ~$5,000/yr |
| ~10 seats | ~$15,000/yr |
| Implementation | ~$2,000-$20,000 one-time |

Plans are quoted; the demo conversation will price your file size and seat count. Budget for implementation, not just the subscription.

## How it compares

[Kindsight](/go/kindsight) and [DonorSearch](/go/donorsearch) rate higher with users, largely because they blend more philanthropic history into their scores. Capacity modeling alone can flag people who could give but likely won't, so plan to read WealthEngine's output next to real giving behavior. For pure large-list speed, it competes well.

## Bottom line

Shortlist it for scale. The useful demo test is the same across the category: bring a sample of donors whose capacity you already know and watch each tool score them. If WealthEngine's model matches reality on people you can verify, its speed on the other fifty thousand records is the payoff.
