Jotform is the workhorse form builder for nonprofit operations: program applications, volunteer intake, scholarship forms, event registrations, board surveys. The free tier covers 5 forms and 100 monthly submissions; paid plans start at $34/month billed annually — and Jotform's standing **50% nonprofit discount** cuts that to roughly $17, which is why it's the rare tool this site recommends without much hand-wringing.

## Who it fits

Any shop the day a program application outgrows an email thread. The capabilities that matter show up fast: conditional logic (show question 12 only if question 4 said yes), file uploads for transcripts and W-9s, payment collection for fee-based programs, and approval workflows that route a submission to the right staffer.

## The honest note

Two disciplines keep Jotform from becoming a liability. First: don't run your donation program through it — form builders can take payments, but a [dedicated giving platform](/donation-processing/) converts better and handles receipts and recurring gifts properly. Second: anything you learn about a donor belongs in the CRM; forms are collection points, not a database of record.
