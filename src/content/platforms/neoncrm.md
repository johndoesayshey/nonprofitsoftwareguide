Neon CRM is a broad donor-management platform that folds donations, events, and memberships into one system. It is worth it for a midsize shop tired of stitching together separate tools — especially membership organizations, where its member features outrun most donor CRMs. The breadth costs you setup time.

## Who it fits

Growing organizations, membership groups, and shops running regular events who want one system instead of three. If you're paying for a separate events tool and a separate membership tool, Neon's consolidation is the pitch.

## What to watch

Feature breadth means onboarding is not a weekend job — plan for it. The interface is denser than lean single-purpose tools. Neon's partner program is relationship-based rather than self-serve, so terms are negotiated. Confirm current pricing tiers against your record count and feature needs.
