Bloomerang is a donor CRM built around retention — its dashboards push you to notice who is lapsing before they're gone. It is worth it for a small-to-midsize shop that wants an approachable database and cares about keeping donors, not just recording gifts. It is priced by record count, so a bloated list costs you.

## Who it fits

Shops replacing spreadsheets or an aging database who want something a non-technical team can actually run. The retention framing is the differentiator: if donor churn is your problem, Bloomerang keeps it visible.

## What to watch

Because pricing scales with records, clean your list before you import — you pay to store lapsed contacts you'll never re-engage. It's a donor CRM, not a grants or program tool; pair it accordingly. Confirm current tier thresholds against your record count.
