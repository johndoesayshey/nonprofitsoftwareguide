Bloomerang is a donor CRM built around retention — its dashboards push lapsing donors in front of you before they're gone. Pricing starts at $79/month for roughly 1,000 contact records (Standard runs ~$125/month), scaling with database size rather than seats, with unlimited users throughout. It's the ease-of-use pick for small-to-midsize teams, with one honest ceiling: power users find its automation and reporting thinner than they'd like.

## Who it fits

Shops where the whole team touches the database and nobody is a data specialist. Bloomerang earns some of the highest ease-of-use marks in the category, and the retention framing changes behavior — when the dashboard leads with "donors about to lapse," follow-up actually happens. If churn is your problem, this is the tool that keeps it visible.

## What it costs

| Tier | Monthly | Records |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | ~$79 | ~1,000 contacts |
| Standard | ~$125 | scales up from there |

The pricing model is the thing to understand: you pay by record count. The practical move practitioners pass along — clean your list **before** you migrate, because you'll pay every month to store lapsed contacts you'll never re-engage, and bills jump at tier breakpoints as the list grows.

## The honest comparison

Against [Little Green Light](/platforms/littlegreenlight/): LGL is roughly half the entry price and more flexible per dollar; Bloomerang is friendlier and stronger on retention analytics. Against [Neon](/platforms/neoncrm/): Neon has more breadth (memberships, events) and more complexity; some teams that outgrow Bloomerang's reporting move there — and some regret trading away the simplicity.

## What to watch

Count your records honestly before you buy, model the bill at your list's realistic size in two years, and test the reports you actually run at board time — that's where the "easy but shallow" critique bites, if it bites.
