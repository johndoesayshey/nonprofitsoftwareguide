Donorbox is donation-processing software: embeddable forms, recurring giving, and donor tools you can launch in an afternoon. It is worth it for a shop that wants to start taking online gifts now without a developer. Watch the fee stack — a platform fee on top of processor fees adds up, and at high volume a flat-rate option may be cheaper.

## Who it fits

Small and midsize shops that need good donation forms fast and value recurring-gift tooling. If your current "donate button" is a mailto link or a clunky processor page, Donorbox is a quick, credible upgrade.
