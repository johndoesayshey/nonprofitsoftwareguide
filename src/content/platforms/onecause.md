OneCause is dedicated event-and-auction software: mobile bidding, live auction management, ticketing, seating charts, and check-in for fundraising events. Entry runs from roughly $200 pay-as-you-go (plus ~5% of funds raised) to annual packages around $2,995/year, with processing fees on top. The buying question is event size: at a $250k signature gala, the fee is insurance; at a $20k community event, it's a free platform's job.

## Who it fits

Shops whose gala is a production: hundreds of guests, a live auctioneer, sponsor tables, a volunteer check-in crew, and a six-figure revenue target on a single night. That night going smoothly is worth real money, and OneCause's machinery — bid paddles to payment in one system — is what you're buying.

## What it costs

| Route | Price |
|---|---|
| Pay-as-you-go | ~$200 entry + ~5% of funds raised |
| Professional auction package | ~$2,995/yr + ~5% platform fee |
| Processing | ~2.9% + 30¢ |
| Implementation / per-event add-ons | $500–$20,000+ depending on scope |

Quotes are custom; make the vendor price your actual event, and ask what the 5% applies to.

## The honest comparison

Against [Givebutter](/platforms/givebutter/): Givebutter runs ticketing and silent auctions free (donor-tip funded), and for most community-scale events it is genuinely enough. OneCause earns its cost when the event needs live-auction support, complex seating, and staff on-site — run the math as a percentage of expected revenue: 5% of $50k stings, 5% of $500k is rounding.

## What to watch

The event platform must not become a shadow database — export every bidder and buyer into your CRM the week after. And there's no self-serve affiliate program here; we cover OneCause because an events category without the category leader would be incomplete.
