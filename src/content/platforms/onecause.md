[OneCause](/go/onecause) is dedicated event-and-auction software: mobile bidding, live auction management, ticketing, seating charts, and check-in. Entry runs from roughly $200 pay-as-you-go (plus about 5% of funds raised) to annual packages around $2,995 a year plus fees. The buying question is event size: for a six-figure signature gala, the fee is insurance on your biggest revenue night.

## Who it fits

Shops whose gala is a production: hundreds of guests, a live auctioneer, sponsor tables, and a volunteer check-in crew. When one night carries a six-figure target, purpose-built machinery from bid paddle to payment is what you're buying, and OneCause is the category leader.

## Pricing

| Route | Price |
|---|---|
| Pay-as-you-go | ~$200 entry + ~5% of funds raised |
| Professional auction package | ~$2,995/yr + platform fee |
| Processing | ~2.9% + 30¢ |
| Implementation / per-event add-ons | varies by scope |

Packages are quoted per event, so have the sales team price your actual gala. The demo is worth it for the mobile-bidding walkthrough alone.

## How it compares

[Givebutter](/go/givebutter) runs ticketing and silent auctions free (donor-tip funded) and covers most community-scale events well. OneCause earns its cost when the event needs live-auction support, complex seating, and on-site staffing. Run the fee as a percentage of expected revenue and the answer usually becomes obvious.

## Bottom line

Size the tool to the event. If your gala clears six figures or is headed there, [book the OneCause demo](/go/onecause) before your next event cycle and price your real program. And whatever platform runs the night, move every bidder into your CRM the week after.
