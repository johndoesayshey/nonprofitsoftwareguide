[Eleo](/go/eleo) is a low-cost donor CRM for small shops: donors, gifts, events, volunteers and mailings in one system, starting at $39 a month for up to 2,500 records. Its real advantage is transparency, since the whole price ladder is published rather than quoted.

## Who it fits

Organizations that want a real database rather than a spreadsheet and are counting every dollar. It sits alongside [Little Green Light](/platforms/littlegreenlight/) as a value pick, and the published pricing makes it easy to budget three years ahead without a sales call.

## Pricing

| Donor records | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Up to 2,500 | $39 |
| Up to 5,000 | $59 |
| Up to 10,000 | $99 |
| Up to 15,000 | $159 |
| Up to 20,000 | $199 |

Above 20,000 records it adds about $10 a month per additional 2,500.

## How it compares

[Little Green Light](/go/littlegreenlight) is the closest match on price and philosophy. [DonorDock](/go/donordock) costs more but drives your daily follow-up with its ActionBoard and carries a 90-day guarantee. [Bloomerang](/go/bloomerang) is friendlier and stronger on retention analytics at a higher price. Clean your list before comparing quotes, since all of them bill by record count.

## Bottom line

Worth a shortlist slot if budget is the binding constraint and you want to know the price for the next three tiers before you commit. One practical note: the site is eleoonline.com, not eleo.com.
