DonorDock is a donor CRM aimed squarely at small shops and solo fundraisers: an approachable database with an action feed that tells you who to reach out to next. It is worth it when your problem is "I have no system and no time," not "I need deep customization." Larger, complex shops will outgrow it.

## Who it fits

The one-person development office, or a small team leaving spreadsheets behind. The action center is the standout — it turns your database into a to-do list instead of a filing cabinet, which is exactly what a stretched solo fundraiser needs.

## What to watch

It's deliberately lean — if you need heavy automation or complex reporting, look higher up the stack. As a smaller vendor, check that its integrations cover your other tools. A reader discount is available; confirm current terms at signup.
