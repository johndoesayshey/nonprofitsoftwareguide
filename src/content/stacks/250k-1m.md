## What to cut first when the budget gets cut

Cut Instrumentl before the CRM. The database holds your donor history — losing it sets you back years, and there's no free substitute once you depend on it. Grant research, painful as it is to drop, can revert to Grants.gov plus manual foundation look-ups for a season. The CRM is the load-bearing wall; the research tool is the nice office.

## When you've outgrown this stack

Move up to the [Established stack]( /stacks/1m-5m/ ) when: you start doing real major-gift work and need wealth screening to prioritize prospects; your CRM's reporting can't answer the questions your board is asking; or grant volume justifies more than a single research seat.
