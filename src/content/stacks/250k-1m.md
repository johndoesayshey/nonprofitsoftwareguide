Crossing $250k is where the first paid tools earn their keep. You've outgrown the all-free stack in two places, donor records and grant prospecting, and this stack spends on exactly those: about $2,700 a year for a real CRM and a real grant pipeline, with processing kept free.

## Why these picks

**CRM: Little Green Light ($45/mo, up to 2,500 constituents).** The small-shop value pick: flexible fields, real reporting, mailing tools, no contracts, and unlimited users. [Little Green Light](/go/littlegreenlight) charges a fraction of what bigger-name systems cost, and the tradeoff is a dated interface. Clean your list before you migrate, and run a test import to confirm gift history and soft credits arrive intact. If your team needs friendlier software to actually use it, [Bloomerang](/go/bloomerang) at $79 a month is the runner-up, and its demo is worth an hour. If you want the database to run your daily follow-up list, look at [DonorDock](/go/donordock) and its 90-day guarantee.

**Grant research: Instrumentl ($179/mo).** The biggest line and the easiest to test. [Instrumentl](/go/instrumentl) earns its price when prospecting is a recurring monthly job: the matching finds funders you wouldn't, and the pipeline tracker keeps deadlines from slipping between two people. The 14-day free trial is the cheap way to decide; load your active applications and see what the matching surfaces. If the budget is tight, [GrantStation](/go/grantstation) through TechSoup at about $199 a year is a tenth the cost for a searchable database without the workflow.

**Processing: Zeffy ($0).** Nothing at this size justifies giving up a percentage of every gift. [Zeffy](/go/zeffy) stays free; revisit when a recurring-giving program or integration need makes [Donorbox's](/go/donorbox) tooling worth its fee.

## What to cut first when the budget gets cut

Cut Instrumentl before the CRM. The database holds your donor history, and losing momentum there sets you back years. Grant research can revert to the free workflow (library research and funder newsletters) for a season; it costs hours, not history. The CRM is the load-bearing wall.

## When you've outgrown this stack

Move up to the [Established stack](/stacks/1m-5m/) when major-gift work starts in earnest and you need wealth screening to focus limited cultivation time, when board questions outrun LGL's reporting, or when grant volume needs more seats and more active projects. That's the jump from $2,700 to five figures, and it should pay for itself in bigger ways.
