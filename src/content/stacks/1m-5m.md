At $1M-$5M the stack becomes infrastructure. Multiple people live in the CRM, grants run as a program rather than a scramble, and major-gift work starts to deserve real research. Budget about $9,500 a year in subscriptions, plus processing fees and the event quote. Every line here should map to a person whose time it protects.

## Why these picks

**CRM: DonorDock Grow (~$300/mo).** The database is now the system of record for three to eight people, and the thing that breaks at this size is not capability, it is habit. [DonorDock](/go/donordock) keeps driving the daily follow-up list while the file grows, and unlimited contacts on every tier means adding 20,000 lapsed names does not re-price your contract, which is exactly what record-count pricing does to you at this stage. Take the demo with your real workflows: gift entry, soft credits, and the board report you run quarterly. Run a test export during the trial, which is basic diligence with any CRM. If retention analytics is the thing your team asks for most, [Bloomerang](/go/bloomerang) is the serious alternative and prices by record count; if memberships and complex event structures drive your model, look at [Neon CRM](/go/neoncrm).

**Grant research: Instrumentl Standard ($299/mo).** Grants are a program now: several staff in the pipeline, ten or more live applications, deadlines that overlap. [Instrumentl's](/go/instrumentl) shared pipeline is what keeps a team-scale calendar from slipping, and the matching keeps the top of the funnel full while everyone is busy writing.

**Money in: Donorbox Pro ($150/mo plus fees).** At this volume recurring-gift management earns its keep. Failed-card recovery and donor portals protect revenue you have already won, and [Donorbox](/go/donorbox) Pro's lower platform fee roughly covers the subscription once online giving passes about $150k.

**Events: Greater Giving (quoted).** A signature auction at this size is a production, and [Greater Giving](/go/greatergiving) sells it as one: mobile bidding, check-in, payment hardware and event-night support in a single package with one vendor accountable. Get the quote early in your event cycle and get the hardware and support terms in writing next to the software price.

**Operations: monday.com (~$432-$720/yr).** Grant deliverables, campaign calendars, event run-of-show. Cheap per seat and the place the work that is not donor data belongs.

## When you've outgrown this stack

Move up to the [Institutional stack](/stacks/5m-plus/) when screening needs to run continuously instead of once a cycle, when the CRM needs real permissions and membership structure, or when a full-time researcher joins the team. That is the point where an annual screening platform stops being a subscription nobody opens.
