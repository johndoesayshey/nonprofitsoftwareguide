At $1M–$5M the stack changes shape: wealth screening enters, because you now have major-gift work worth prioritizing, and the CRM becomes the system the whole team lives in. This is the tier where skipping prospect research actively costs you — you're leaving major gifts unfound. The line items are real money now; each one should map to a person whose time it protects.

## What to cut first when the budget gets cut

Cut prospect research first. It's the newest line and the most deferrable — you can run last year's screen for another cycle, or fall back to hand research on your top 50. The CRM and processing are operational; grant research funds the shortfall you're trying to close. Screening is leverage, and leverage is what you trim when you're protecting the core.

## When you've outgrown this stack

Move up to the [Institutional stack]( /stacks/5m-plus/ ) when: you're running large events and auctions that need dedicated software; your CRM can't model the reporting and permissions a bigger team needs; or your screening cadence goes from annual to continuous.
