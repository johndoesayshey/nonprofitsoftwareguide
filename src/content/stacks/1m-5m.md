At $1M-$5M the stack changes shape: wealth screening enters, because you now have major-gift work worth prioritizing, and the CRM becomes the system the whole team lives in. Budget $11,000-$14,000 a year all-in. Every line item here should map to a person whose time it protects.

## Why these picks

**CRM: Bloomerang (~$125/mo at typical database sizes).** At this size the whole team touches the database, so adoption beats horsepower: the CRM your staff actually updates wins. [Bloomerang's](/go/bloomerang) retention dashboards put lapsing donors in front of whoever logs in, and unlimited seats mean growth doesn't change the bill. If your board reporting or membership model outgrows it, [Neon CRM](/go/neoncrm) adds real breadth; take its demo with your actual workflows and run a test export during the trial, a good habit with any CRM you evaluate.

**Prospect research: DonorSearch (quoted; reported $4,000-$6,000/yr).** The new line, with one condition attached: a named person turns the results into portfolios and visits. [DonorSearch](/go/donorsearch) leads with demonstrated giving history, which experienced researchers weight over raw wealth markers. Book demos with DonorSearch and [Kindsight](/go/kindsight) and ask each to score a sample of donors you already know; watching them score people you can verify is the fastest evaluation there is.

**Grant research: Instrumentl Standard ($299/mo).** Grants are a program now: multiple staff in the pipeline and ten or more active applications. [Instrumentl's](/go/instrumentl) shared deadline pipeline is what keeps a team-scale calendar from slipping.

**Processing: Donorbox Pro ($150/mo).** At this volume, recurring-gift management earns its keep: failed-card recovery and donor portals protect monthly revenue you already earned. [Donorbox](/go/donorbox) Pro's lower platform fee roughly covers its own subscription at $150k+ of online giving.

## What to cut first when the budget gets cut

Cut prospect research first: run last year's screen another cycle, or hand-research your top 50. Then step Donorbox Pro down to the free tier. Protect the CRM and grant research last; one holds your history, the other funds the shortfall you're trying to close.

## When you've outgrown this stack

Move up to the [Institutional stack](/stacks/5m-plus/) when screening needs to run continuously instead of annually, when your signature event outgrows free tooling, or when the CRM needs real permissions and membership structure.
