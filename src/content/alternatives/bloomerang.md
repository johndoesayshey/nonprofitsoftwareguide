Bloomerang is a well-built system, and most searches for alternatives are driven by one of two things: the record-count bill, or a feature it was never designed to carry. Both have clean answers, and they aren't the same answer.

## First, check whether you have a pricing problem or a data problem

Bloomerang bills on contact records. Before you price a migration, export your contact list and count what's actually in it. A typical file that grew organically over eight years carries duplicates, deceased constituents, bounced email-only signups and everyone who ever bought a gala ticket. Cleaning that is a week of work and it frequently drops an organization a full pricing tier. A migration is a quarter of work. Do the cheap thing first.

If you clean the file and the number still doesn't work, the structural fix is flat-tier pricing with unlimited contacts. That is [DonorDock](/go/donordock)'s pricing model, and it's the reason it shows up first here.

## What you actually give up

Be honest about the retention reporting. Bloomerang's signature feature is that lapsing-donor visibility sits on the home screen instead of behind a report build. Every alternative on this page can tell you who lapsed, none of them puts it in front of you unasked. If your team is disciplined enough to run the query, that's fine. If the only reason anyone knows the retention number is that Bloomerang displays it, expect that number to quietly stop being tracked after the migration.

The second thing you give up is training cost. Bloomerang is consistently rated among the easiest systems in the category to learn. DonorDock is comparable. Little Green Light is not, and Eleo sits in between.

## The cost comparison that matters

Our 2026 survey of 100 U.S. fundraising professionals put mean annual donor CRM spend at $716 for shops under $250K raised, $2,726 at $250K–$1M, and $7,130 at $1M–$5M. Bloomerang CRM runs roughly $1,500 a year, or $1,980 with the Fundraising module where the donation forms live. That is comfortably inside the band at $250K–$1M and above it under $250K, which is the size where most alternative searches originate, and where Eleo or Little Green Light close the gap without giving up much that a small shop uses.

Whatever you choose, ask the new vendor to import your real export before you sign, and check that soft credits and household relationships survive. Those two fields are where donor CRM migrations lose the history your major-gift work depends on.
