# Survey outreach shortlist

Built 2026-08-03 from a sweep of ~75 nonprofit newsletters, blogs, podcasts and
association publications. Every outlet listed was loaded and confirmed live; dates
are the most recent post actually visible on the page.

**The finding that shapes everything below: for an anonymous publisher, guest
posting is mostly closed and citation is wide open.** Nearly every guest channel
demands a named human — Nonprofit Tech for Good's guest page literally opens
"Want to grow your personal brand", NTEN requires paid membership, NPQ requires
bylined essays, Blue Avocado wants first-person lived experience. But research
roundups, news desks and resource pages credit **publications**, not people.

So: pitch the data, never the byline.

## Tier 1 — the eight to work first

### 1. Nonprofit Tech for Good — the research roundup ⭐ best single fit
**I verified this one personally.** The roundup is real, recurring and current:
`nptechforgood.com/2026/06/17/digital-marketing-fundraising-research-reports-for-nonprofits/`
— dated **17 June 2026**, listing 20 reports **by publication name, no author
bylines**, and it already includes small independents (AI Equity Project) beside
Blackbaud and M+R. Earlier editions: 2020 (26 reports), 2018 (22).

**Reach is the largest verified in the sector:** 740k X, 110k FB, 77k IG, 23k LI.

**The opening:** I read all 20 entries. They cover email, monthly giving, social,
AI adoption, digital trends. **Not one covers software spending or pricing.** That
is a real gap in their own list and it is exactly what your survey is. Pitch it as
filling a hole, not as another sector benchmark.

Contact: `heather@nptechforgood.com`. Their guest-post page is a paid/branding
track — ignore it. Time the pitch before next June's edition.

### 2. NonProfit PRO — the Editor-in-Chief writes these up herself
Amanda Cole personally covered "M+R Benchmarks 2026" on 23 Apr 2026. A named
editor with a public address who already does exactly this.
Contact: `acole@columbiabooks.com`

### 3. National Council of Nonprofits — resource page
Their fundraising resource page currently links a donor-management software
buyer's guide from **2020**. No bylines — these are unattributed link entries. The
most-linked nonprofit resource library in the US, `.org` authority.
Route: site contact form. *(Their site 403s automated fetches; use a browser.)*

### 4. Whole Whale / Nonprofit News Feed — one pitch, two placements
5,000+ subscribers, and the newsletter feeds a podcast.
Contact: `NPNewsFeed@wholewhale.com`

⚠️ Their agency contact page says they do not accept guest posts or link
exchanges and will not reply. Use the NPNewsFeed address only, framed as data.

### 5. DH Leonard — annual roundup with an open invitation
Their "Top Grant Writing Podcasts and Blogs" roundup links 15 outside outlets and
ends by **inviting you to email for next year's list**. Credits publications.
Grants beat, which is your deepest content.
Contact: `Megan@dhleonardconsulting.com`

### 6. GivingTuesday Data Commons — the only zero-conflict research body
Sells nothing. 180 data partners, 2,000 collaborators, publicly invites
researchers to collaborate.
Contact: `media@givingtuesday.org`

### 7. RKD Group — "Rossi's Roundup"
A standing monthly column whose entire job is rounding up new industry data.
Contact: Lisa Rossi via `connect@rkdgroup.com`

### 8. Missions to Movements (Dana Snyder) — no guest needed
The one podcast that builds **solo** episodes around third-party reports — e.g.
"What 1,000 Donors Are Telling You: Key Takeaways from 2026 Giving Signals
Report", 24 Jun 2026. You never have to appear.
Contact: `kat@positiveequation.com`

## The conflict list — do not pitch

**Donorbox is a disclosure problem, not just a conflict.** They cite M+R
Benchmarks and would plausibly cover the survey — but they are a Tier A affiliate
partner you have applied to. Coverage from a vendor you monetize is a different
category of problem from a competitor declining to link you. Leave it.

**Instrumentl** — highest conflict of all: your top content category, an affiliate
partner, and a competitor to Candid whom you also cover.

**Vendor blogs generally** — Bloomerang, Virtuous, DonorPerfect, CharityEngine,
Givebutter, Blackbaud Institute. You rank their products. They will not link a
comparison naming a competitor as the better buy.

**Nonprofit Quarterly** — pays $300–500 but the guidelines reject press releases
and self-promotion and require bylined essays. Structurally incompatible.

**Blue Avocado** — wants first-person lived experience and states "AI-generated
content is prohibited. (We'll know.)" Good outlet, wrong author fit.

**Vu Le / Nonprofit AF** — verified: he does not link outside research and his
About page rules out guest posts. Big audience, wrong mechanism.

## Two notes worth keeping

**Audience numbers barely exist in this sector.** Across ~75 outlets only seven
published a real figure. Do not rank targets by stated size — there is nothing to
rank on. Rank by whether they have visibly cited outside data before.

**Three outlets already publish research that overlaps yours** — NTEN's Donor
Management Systems Survey, Nonprofit Marketing Guide's Communications Trends
Report, and Nonprofit Tech for Good's own annual report. That is both why they
would care and why they might not. Lead with the angle none of them covers:
**actual dollar spend by shop size.** Complementary data, not a competing survey.
